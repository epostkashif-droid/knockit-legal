Knockit privacy policy

Knockit: To-Do & Planner, for Android and Wear OS · Effective 15 November 2026

Knockit is a to-do list and planner. It is built so that your tasks belong to you. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What Knockit stores

The tasks, notes, sub-tasks, categories, reminders and completion statistics you create. They are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

What Knockit does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics code.
  • No account with the developer, and no developer server. Your data is never sent to the developer.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Your data is never sold or shared.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you switch on Drive sync, Knockit saves a copy of your tasks to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Knockit can use (Google's "app data" folder). Knockit asks only for permission to that folder and cannot see any other file in your Drive. The copy travels directly between your phone and Google over an encrypted connection. Your use of Google Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings, and remove Knockit's access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.

Wear OS

If you use the watch app, your phone sends today's tasks to your paired watch using Google's Wear OS connection. The data stays on your own devices.

Backups you export

If you export a backup, Knockit writes a file to the location you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything erases all your Knockit data from your phone and, if Drive sync was on, from your Google Drive. Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored on your phone.

Children

Knockit is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
