TokenLive privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. TokenLive is made by Kashif Nowman Kanak, Chennai, India. Contact: epost.kashif@gmail.com.

What the clinic app sends

To show the live queue, the TokenLive app sends the clinic name, the doctor's name, the current token number, the number of tokens given, an estimated minutes-per-patient, the doctor's status (consulting, break, not in yet, closed) and the time of the update. These go through the public ntfy.sh relay service to the patient page, on a random channel unique to the clinic. Anyone with the clinic's link or QR code can see this information.

What TokenLive does not collect

No patient names. Patient mobile numbers typed in the app are used only to open WhatsApp or SMS on the clinic's own phone and are not stored or sent by TokenLive. No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no location.

Patient page

The patient page stores the patient's token number and language choice in the patient's own browser. It receives queue updates from ntfy.sh. If the patient allows notifications, the page uses them only to say their turn is near.

On the clinic's phone

Settings and session history stay in the app's private storage and are not included in Android backups.
